    What shapes household wellbeing?

    Food and warmth outlooks reflect town reserves and market firewood availability, not individual purchases. Care pressure reflects the ages of people living together. Wellbeing starts at 70/100 on upgrade, changes daily and recovers gradually. These provisional indicators add no consumption, charges or extra deaths.

    Settled: 65–100 · Under strain: 35–64 · Struggling: 0–34. Children under six need more care; residents aged 75 and over need support. These are simple simulation assumptions, not individual health assessments.
