How the clock works

The named Gregorian-length calendar runs at one real second per Arcanus second. It catches up on opening, importing or returning. Pause freezes time, including while closed. Daily economic steps still settle in legacy 30-day accounting periods.

    Parents, partners, children and guardians appear beneath each resident. Initial links are provisional, based on plausible age gaps within existing households; unknown parentage stays unrecorded. No past weddings or births are invented.

    Grown children can establish homes from ages 20–24 when dependants have care. At most two new homes form per month; relatives and members of the same original household do not become partners.     What shapes wellbeing?

    Food and warmth outlooks reflect town reserves and market firewood, not individual purchases. Care pressure reflects residents’ ages. Low reserve warnings carry mild penalties; serious shortage risk carries more weight. Cold affects households with young children or older residents more strongly. Wellbeing changes daily and recovers gradually. Settled: 65–100 · Under strain: 35–64 · Struggling: 0–34.

    Children under six need more care; residents aged 75 and over need support. These provisional simulation assumptions add no consumption, charges or extra deaths.

    How the inn trades

    The owner-manager is one of the existing inn workers, included within the eight-person limit. If they retire or leave, an eligible adult worker takes over provisionally. Without an adult proprietor, trading pauses; upkeep continues.

    Visits cover drinks and hospitality, not extra household meals. Demand changes with town population, season, rest days and household wellbeing. Each worker supports six visits per day. A visit earns 15 cp and costs 4 cp in trade supplies; daily wages are 30 cp per working staff member, including the owner, plus 20 cp of upkeep. 100 cp = 1 gp.

    The inn pays its own bills; the town Treasury, staple food and timber stores are unchanged. Private customer spending and external supplies are estimated; personal wallets are not yet tracked. Cash can become debt. There is no automatic financing, bankruptcy or charged inheritance.

    Accounts begin at the saved simulation day when this version is first loaded. No earlier inn trading is invented. Names, ownership and rates are provisional, not established Arcanus canon.
