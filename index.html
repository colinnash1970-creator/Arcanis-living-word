How the clock works

1 real second = 1 Arcanus second. Time follows the UK clock, including daylight saving. The date advances at UK midnight, with provisional 30-day months and monthly economy updates. Closing the app is fine: it catches up when you return. Pausing freezes routines and skips elapsed time on resume.

    Family register

    Parents, partners, children and guardians appear beneath each resident. Initial links are provisional, based on plausible age gaps within existing households; unknown parentage stays unrecorded. No past weddings or births are invented.

    Grown children can establish homes from ages 20–24 when dependants have care. At most two new homes form per month; relatives and members of the same original household do not become partners. Residents keep their names and jobs. A new household represents a new home arrangement, not a paid construction project.

    Starting children need several years to reach this stage. Births and deaths still follow the existing town economy. Family updates are grouped into one monthly Chronicle entry.

    Occupations across town

    The single generic town inn has up to eight staff, provisionally. Other non-timber jobs describe broad sectors; individual shops and inns are not yet business simulations.

    What shapes wellbeing?

    Food and warmth outlooks reflect town reserves and market firewood, not individual purchases. Care pressure reflects residents’ ages. Low reserve warnings carry mild penalties; serious shortage risk carries more weight. Cold affects households with young children or older residents more strongly. Wellbeing changes daily and recovers gradually. Settled: 65–100 · Under strain: 35–64 · Struggling: 0–34.

    Children under six need more care; residents aged 75 and over need support. These provisional simulation assumptions add no consumption, charges or extra deaths.

    About daily life

    Residents, households and occupations are saved. Routines follow UK time and freeze while paused. The seventh town day is a rest day with essential morning duties. Names and assignments are provisional; household scenes add no wages, food use or timber sales.
