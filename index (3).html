Calendar conventions & sources

Weekdays follow Monday → Sunday: Lunareth, Daneth, Stoneward, Stormwake, Kielday, Starwatch, Dawnrest. Deepwatch starts at midnight; the other watches follow every three hours.

828 AF uses the 2026 astronomical season boundaries. Other years repeat those month/day/time boundaries as an explicitly approximate seasonal template. Southern seasons are reversed. No climate, sunrise, sunset or geographic locations are inferred.

Aeloria’s fixed 29.53059-day cycle is anchored to the new moon on 18 January 2026 at 19:52 UTC. Its illumination is a mean-cycle estimate. Historical back-projection is approximate, not an Earth ephemeris.

Year 0 AF exists and maps to 1198 CE. Supported range: 0–8801 AF. Dates use the proleptic Gregorian calendar. February 29 events are skipped in common years. Historical records and known birthdays do not recur before their origin or birth year. Sula’s birth year remains undetermined.
