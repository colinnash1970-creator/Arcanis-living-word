# Stage 5 — Volume II audit

Volume II, The Foundations of Creation, was inspected in the complete 103-page Grand Chronicle: combined PDF pages 11–21, corresponding to Volume II pages 1–11. Text was extracted and the substantive pages 13–20 were visually checked. This release expands the existing v0.2 extraction, not all twelve volumes.

## Result

38 new records; 8 existing records enriched; 1 relationship added. Database v0.3 contains 35 characters, 28 locations, 8 factions, 26 events and 10 world-mechanics records: 107 searchable records, plus 30 relationships and 5 review issues.

All 69 original records and their existing fields are retained. Eight receive additional, separately sourced reference notes. The original 29 relationships are retained, and the explicitly stated twin relationship between Kielinya and Danel adds one. Malcolm’s Brightblade birth name, later Darkbane name and royal succession distinctions are preserved.

## Canon decisions and uncertainties

No direct contradiction requiring a change to an existing v0.2 field was found in the reviewed material. The main unresolved issue is the completion/play status of the 793–828 reconstruction sequence. Its stated facts are retained, but it is not counted as completed history. Other open items are deliberate source limits, not errors to fill with invented lore.

### Distinct entities
The Eight Realms are realm records, not duplicates of the eight Kielinstrian province records. Arcanium and Dracarnia are outside the Eight-school set.

### Duplicate events avoided
Unification, Sternia’s destruction and the planned Kaddinghall battle retain their original IDs. White Flight’s planned intervention enriches the existing battle rather than creating a second battle event.

### Two Keys
The First Key and Eighteenfold/Second Key have separate records and purposes. The developing altar is marked planned.

### Reconstruction sequence
The 793–828 range is stored as a range. Completion status is unresolved; participants are not invented. The Observer identity is preserved as a source assertion.

### Sternia timing
The existing annual state rules remain intact. Volume II source notes supply the guardian context without silently revising the prior after-793 condition field.

### Lost Ages
The broad mythic sequence supplies context, not fabricated individual dates for divine awakenings, guardianship or settlements.

### Shattered Veil
Its distinction from the lattice and unsealed campaign status are reference facts, not an invented dated transition.

## Timeline safety

No automatic world-state effects were added. The original precisely dated events already cover the source’s established 0 and 793 A.F. events. Newly added events are undated, approximate, planned or unresolved. Sula’s approximately 10,000 and 9,994 B.F. dates remain approximate. The seven-years-per-Arcanium-day relationship is a setting reference, not a replacement for the town clock.

## Open questions

### Has the reconstruction sequence been played?
Page 16 describes the five-day / 35-year sequence; page 20 keeps the 828 finale planned. These do not prove whether this separate sequence has already been played. Preserve its stated range and identity revelation, but do not count it as completed history without a canon decision.

Combined PDF pages 16, 20. A canon decision is needed before treating the sequence as completed.

### Ancient dates remain approximate or unknown
No precise First Dawn or Krynn-destruction year is supplied; Sula’s ancient dates are approximate. Do not manufacture precise dates or derive new ones from elapsed time.

Combined PDF pages 13, 16, 20. No decision is required to leave this unknown.

### Final altar design remains developing
The design is a developing prop/scenario element, not a confirmed physical design or dated historical construction.

Combined PDF pages 14. No decision is required to leave this unknown.

### First Law edge cases are not settled
Do not extend the rule to every god, outside visitor or artefact without a separate canon decision.

Combined PDF pages 18. No decision is required to leave this unknown.

### Island name and position are not recorded
The planned Syphon’s island Nexus is linked directly to the Arcanium, but no island name or map position is supplied in this volume.

Combined PDF pages 16, 17. No decision is required to leave this unknown.
